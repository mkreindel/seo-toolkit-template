# Unique opening (≥ 200 words, city-specific)

Tapas catering in Houston has shifted noticeably in the past two years. The corporate market that used to default to Tex-Mex platters or barbecue trays is increasingly asking for Spanish small plates — partly because Houston's downtown event scene (Discovery Green, the Hobby Center, Hotel Alessandra) has matured into spaces that suit the format, and partly because Houston's restaurant scene itself has normalized tapas at the level it took New York and LA five years longer to reach.

Our Houston bookings broke down roughly 60% corporate / 30% private celebrations / 10% weddings in 2025 — different from our Katy and Sugar Land mix where weddings dominate. The corporate-heavy Houston market means our standard tapas format (8 small plates passed-style, 90-minute service window) needs to flex for finance-firm receptions in the Galleria district, tech-launch events in EaDo, and law-firm partner dinners in the Heights — three completely different rooms with three completely different timing rhythms.

The Houston detail that catches most clients off guard: parking and load-in logistics at downtown venues add 30–60 minutes versus suburban events. We build that into the quote up front. The Houston detail that doesn't change: a good tapas spread holds the room for 90 minutes whether you're in Sugar Land or Midtown.

# City-specific details

**Houston-typical event venues we've catered (last 24 months):**
- Discovery Green (corporate launches, 80–180 guests)
- Hotel Alessandra rooftop (private celebrations, 30–80 guests)
- The Heights private homes (intimate birthdays, 12–40 guests)
- Memorial-area private residences (50–100 guests)
- Hobby Center pre-show receptions (varies)
- The Astorian (weddings + corporate, 100–200 guests)

**Houston-typical dietary considerations:**
- Roughly 1 in 3 Houston events requests vegetarian options matching the percentage of plant-forward guests
- Halal accommodations more frequently requested than in suburban markets
- Houston's Indian-American business community often books events with no-pork constraints — we maintain a parallel tapas menu for these

**Houston pricing context:**
- Houston downtown events run 15–20% higher than suburban events due to load-in time, parking, and union venue requirements at some spaces
- Quoted per-guest pricing assumes Inner Loop or downtown; suburban pricing is quoted separately
