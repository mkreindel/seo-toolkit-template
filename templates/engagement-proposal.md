## Executive summary

Three sentences max. What the engagement delivers, in plain language, with the headline outcome the client cares about.

> Example: We'll run an SEO engagement for [Client] focused on increasing organic conversions from the AI consulting product line. Phase 1 (one month) delivers the strategy: keyword research, web architecture, technical audit, and 90-day roadmap. Phase 2 (recurring monthly) executes the roadmap, ships the content, and reports against a measurable KPI sheet you'll see in section 5.

## 2. Phase 1 — Discovery + strategy (one-time, ~1 month)

### Deliverables

- Discovery interview synthesized to a written brief
- **Keyword research** — transactional keyword list (commercial intent) + informational keyword list (blog topics)
- **Transactional web architecture** — Level 0 (homepage) → Level 1 (categories) → Level 2 (subcategories) hierarchy with monthly volumes
- **Technical SEO audit** — 8 non-negotiables, Core Web Vitals (mobile), schema, foundational infrastructure (GSC, GA4, GBP if local), rendering audit if CSR/hybrid stack
- **KPI baseline + targets** — see section 5 + the `kpis.md` template that pairs with this engagement
- **30 / 60 / 90-day roadmap** — sequenced tasks, owners, dependencies
- **Strategy presentation** — 60-min walkthrough with you and the team, recorded

### Timeline

- Week 1: kickoff + discovery
- Week 2: keyword research + architecture
- Weeks 3–4: technical audit + KPI baseline + roadmap
- End of week 4: strategy presentation

## 3. Phase 2 — Implementation + monitoring (recurring monthly)

### Monthly deliverables

- Implementation of roadmap items scheduled for the month
- **Content production:** [N blog posts / month] + [N service or category pages / month]
- Technical fixes from the `/audit` punchlist
- **Monthly progress report:** GSC clicks / impressions / positions / indexed pages, AI search citation status, Core Web Vitals trend, backlinks gained, conversion data
- One review call (30 min) to align on next month's priorities

### Cadence

- Mid-month: status async update via [email / Slack / etc.]
- End-of-month: report + review call
- Quarterly: KPI comparison vs targets + scope adjustment if needed

### Phase 2 fee

**[Currency + Amount] / month** — recurring, billed at start of month.

(Sized at ~50–75% of Phase 1 per `SEO_GUIDE.md` Section 10.1. Adjust by catalog size, content cadence, market count.)

### Engagement length

Open-ended monthly retainer with **30-day notice** by either side. No long-term contract. The work compounds — most clients see meaningful results at 90 days, breakthrough at 6–9 months.

## 4. What's NOT included

Listed up front so scope is clear. The engagement does NOT include:

- Paid search (Google Ads / Bing Ads)
- Paid social
- Email marketing / newsletter setup
- Influencer outreach
- Site redesign or platform migration
- Custom development beyond toolkit-supported CMS edits
- Ongoing graphic design beyond what `/blog` and `/service` skills produce
- Legal / compliance review of content
- [Other explicit exclusions specific to this engagement]

If any of the above becomes needed mid-engagement, it gets a separate proposal.

## 5. KPIs + measurement cadence

Pairs with `templates/kpis.md` — the per-site KPI sheet we'll fill in Phase 1.

| Horizon | Targets |
|---|---|
| **90 days** | Organic clicks +50%, impressions +100%, ≥1 AI citation, [conversion goal] |
| **180 days** | Clicks +100%, ≥5 AI citations, DR +5 from baseline |
| **365 days** | Clicks +200%, top-3 ranking on 3 commercial keywords |

Targets are conservative-realistic. Set together at end of Phase 1 with full visibility into the baseline. Reviewed quarterly.

## 6. What success looks like

- **At 90 days:** organic traffic visibly up, the first AI search citation lands, the site is technically sound, content cadence is consistent.
- **At 180 days:** ranking gains on commercial keywords, multiple AI citations, conversion path measurable.
- **At 365 days:** organic is a meaningful acquisition channel — top-3 ranking on 3+ commercial keywords, AI search citations sustained, conversion volume that compounds.

## Notes for the consultant (delete before sending)

- Read `SEO_GUIDE.md` Section 10 before pricing — bands are reference points, not quotes; price the engagement against actual catalog size + market count + risk profile.
- Don't over-promise targets in section 5. Lost client at month 3 due to overshoot is worse than no client.
- Section 4 ("What's NOT included") is the most important. Every engagement that goes sideways does so because something not-explicitly-excluded gets demanded mid-stream.
- The toolkit's `/audit`, `/blog`, `/service`, `/refresh`, `/triage`, and `/wireframe` skills cover the operational delivery. The proposal is the agreement that makes those skills' output sellable.
