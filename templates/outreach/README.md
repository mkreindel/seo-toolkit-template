# Outreach templates

Pitch templates for the 5 outreach types surfaced by the P2.1 discovery skills (`/haro`, `/broken-backlinks`, `/competitor-backlinks`). Each template is the canonical starting point — the actual pitch is voice-customized per site by drafting against the site's `references/voice.md` + `stats.md` + `stories.md`.

## When to use which template

| Outreach type | Template | Surfaced by | Conversion rate (industry baseline) |
|---------------|----------|-------------|-------------------------------------|
| HARO / journalist response | `haro.md` | `/haro` | 5–15% (depending on outlet + match quality) |
| Broken-link replacement | `broken-link.md` | `/broken-backlinks` | 8–15% (highest of cold outreach types) |
| Resource-page inclusion | `resource-page.md` | `/competitor-backlinks` (hook=resource-page) | 3–8% |
| Editorial mention / expert quote ask | `expert-quote.md` | `/competitor-backlinks` (hook=editorial / expert-quote) | 2–5% |
| Guest-post pitch | `guest-post.md` | `/competitor-backlinks` (hook=guest-post) | 1–4% (lower volume, higher quality) |

## Universal rules (apply to every template)

- **First 50 words decide the open.** If the publisher doesn't see relevance in the first 50 words, they delete. Lead with the specific reason this pitch is relevant to THEIR page, not a generic intro about the site.
- **No fake personalization.** "I loved your article on X" with no specifics screams template. If you can't cite something specific the publisher said, skip the praise.
- **No links in cold pitches.** Reporters + editorial publishers explicitly mark URL-bearing first-touch emails as spam. The link goes in the follow-up after they reply asking for more info.
- **One ask per email.** Pitching multiple URLs / multiple inclusions / multiple favors in one email tanks response rate.
- **Real signature with phone + role + URL.** Soft-credibility signal that the sender exists. Email-only sig is a spam pattern.
- **Voice-match.** Pitch tone matches the site's `references/voice.md`. AI-Beacon brand voice ≠ Site Bsf founder voice ≠ Site C medical-authority voice.
- **No `Send from` mismatches.** The display name + from-address must match the byline policy on the site. (Site A outreach goes from a real human on the team, NOT "Site A" — per byline policy memory, brand-as-author only applies to ON-SITE bylines.)
- **Follow-up cadence per `backlinks.md` outreach pipeline:** 14 days → follow-up #1, 21 days → follow-up #2 (final), 30 days → mark abandoned. No more than 2 follow-ups.

## Subject line guidelines

Subject lines convert at 35–45% open rate for outreach when they pass these tests:
- ≤ 8 words
- Specific to the recipient (mentions their domain, their article title, their topic)
- Implies value to them, not to you ("found broken link on your X tools page" > "request for backlink")
- No `[PITCH]`, `[REACHOUT]`, `[BACKLINK REQUEST]` brackets — instant delete

## Pre-send checklist

Before any pitch leaves, the sender verifies:
- [ ] Recipient is the right person (not a generic `editor@` if a specific editor exists)
- [ ] Recipient domain isn't in `backlinks.md` "Banned outreach targets"
- [ ] Recipient hasn't been pitched in the last 90 days (cross-check `backlinks.md` "Outreach response tracking")
- [ ] The pitch references something specific about THEIR page
- [ ] The pitch contains zero URLs
- [ ] The signature has phone + role + URL
- [ ] The send-from name matches site voice/byline policy
- [ ] Outreach pipeline row appended to `backlinks.md` with status=`sent` BEFORE sending

## What this template set does NOT do

- It does not automate sending. Per-pitch user approval is the floor. Mass-sending these templates without review produces spam patterns Google can detect on the receiving end.
- It does not handle reply-to-conversation. Once a publisher replies, this is human work — the value of the relationship per-conversation exceeds any template's value.
